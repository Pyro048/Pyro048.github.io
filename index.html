Why I built it

From “chatbot” to dependable system.

I wanted to understand what makes an AI workflow useful beyond a single prompt. That meant working through orchestration, specialization, memory, resource limits, verification, and the operational details that make the system understandable when something goes wrong.

Design decisions
  • 01
    Specialize the workAgents are organized around clear responsibilities instead of overlapping everything.
  • 02
    Route with intentionTasks can be matched to models by complexity, capability, and cost.
  • 03
    Make state usefulPersistent memory and knowledge support continuity across sessions.
  • 04
    Design for visibilityLogs, dashboards, and verification make behavior easier to inspect.

Standardizing IT Troubleshooting

At IB-Supply, I recognized that recurring support issues needed a more consistent response. I created a standardized troubleshooting checklist to guide diagnosis, capture what had already been tried, and reduce repeat problems.

Intervention

Value
