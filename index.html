Why I built it

From isolated prompts to agent operations.

I wanted an operating layer where agent roles, task routing, recovery behavior, tests, and dashboards were explicit. Paperclip became a hands-on environment for building dependable workflows instead of relying on an opaque chain of prompts.

Design decisions
  • 01
    Give every agent a jobManagement, engineering, research, and worker roles have clear ownership.
  • 02
    Pin routes to intentCritical engineering work is directed to the right role and model route.
  • 03
    Recover quietlyProvider outages enter a waiting state with bounded retries and cooldowns.
  • 04
    Require evidenceReplay tests and default-off safety gates prevent weak experiments from being promoted.
↗Agent fleetCoordinate five agents with distinct operational roles.
⌘Role-pinned routingSend work to the correct role and configured provider route.
◫Provider recoveryPause safely, bound retries, and resume when services return.
